# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FlopBench a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, evidence-driven, and mindful that benchmark results can be sensitive. Do not pressure contributors to disclose private machine, account, wallet, or identity data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, deceptive performance claims, and promotion of FlopBench as an official FLOP or reward-eligibility tool are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this code. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
