## Corrections made during this audit

explicit `S0-T01` through `S0-T06` traceability. The wording and task runners
were corrected, contract coverage was added, and a retrospective acceptance
record was created.

retrospective acceptance records. Records were added after their source,
hardware, rules, privacy, consent, cleanup, and live acceptance checks passed.

No product-code correction was required in Stages 1 through 10. Historical
acceptance records remain historical snapshots and are not silently rewritten
to imply that later stages existed at their original acceptance dates.
